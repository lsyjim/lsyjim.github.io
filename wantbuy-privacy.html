欲望清單 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 24 日

繁體中文English

「欲望清單」（以下稱「本 App」）由 Lai Jiun Yung 開發。本政策說明本 App 如何處理您的資料。

1. 我們不會收集您清單裡的內容

您輸入的名稱、價格、照片、地點與連結，只儲存在您的裝置上；若您的裝置已登入 iCloud，這些資料會透過 Apple 的 iCloud（CloudKit 私人資料庫）同步到您登入同一個 Apple 帳號的其他裝置。開發者無法存取您 iCloud 裡的資料。

  • 相機與照片：只在您選擇拍照或挑選照片時使用，照片會壓縮後存入您的清單，不會上傳到開發者的伺服器。
  • 定位：只在您按下「取得目前地址」時使用一次，用來轉成地址文字填入欄位，不會在背景追蹤位置，也不會傳給開發者。
  • 本 App 沒有帳號系統，也沒有開發者自己的伺服器。

2. 廣告（Google AdMob）

本 App 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。AdMob 可能會收集並使用下列資料來提供、衡量及個人化廣告：

  • 裝置識別碼（例如廣告識別碼 IDFA，僅在您於「App 追蹤透明度」視窗允許後才會取得）
  • IP 位址與由此推估的大略位置
  • 裝置與系統資訊、廣告互動資料、當機與效能資料

歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者，會在第一次開啟時看到 Google 的同意聲明畫面，可隨時在 App 的「設定 → 廣告隱私設定」更改選擇。您也可以在 iPhone 的「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」關閉追蹤。拒絕追蹤不影響 App 的任何功能，只會看到非個人化廣告。

Google 如何使用資料，請參考：Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料、Google 隱私權政策。

3. 資料保存與刪除

您可以隨時在 App 內刪除單筆資料，或在「設定 → 清空所有資料」一次刪除全部（已同步的 iCloud 資料也會一起刪除）。刪除 App 會移除裝置上的資料；iCloud 上的資料可在 iPhone 的「設定 → [您的名字] → iCloud → 管理儲存空間」中刪除。

4. 兒童

本 App 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。

5. 政策變更與聯絡方式

本政策如有變更，會更新於本頁並修改上方日期。有任何問題請來信：lsyjim@hotmail.com

WantBuy Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

WantBuy ("the App") is developed by Lai Jiun Yung. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. We do not collect your list

The names, prices, photos, locations and links you enter are stored only on your device. If your device is signed in to iCloud, they are synced through Apple iCloud (CloudKit private database) to your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. The developer cannot access your iCloud data.

  • Camera and photos are used only when you choose to take or pick a photo. Photos are compressed and stored in your list; they are never uploaded to the developer.
  • Location is used once, only when you tap "Use current address", to fill in an address. It is not tracked in the background and is not sent to the developer.
  • The App has no user accounts and no developer-operated servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect and use the following to serve, measure and personalize ads:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier IDFA, only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and system information, ad interaction data, crash and performance data

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland will see Google's consent message on first launch and can change their choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the App. You can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining does not limit any feature; you will see non-personalized ads.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Retention and deletion

You can delete any item in the App, or delete everything in Settings → Clear All Data (synced iCloud data is deleted as well). Deleting the App removes on-device data; iCloud data can be removed in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
