就是你了 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 24 日

繁體中文English

「就是你了」（以下稱「本 App」）由 Lai Jiun Yung 開發。本政策說明本 App 如何處理您的資料。

1. 您在 App 裡輸入的內容

您建立的名單、選項與設定只儲存在您的裝置上，不會傳送到開發者的伺服器。本 App 沒有帳號系統，也沒有開發者自己的伺服器。

2. App 內購買

完整版透過 Apple 的 App 內購買（StoreKit）處理。付款與帳單資訊由 Apple 處理，開發者不會取得您的付款資料，只會收到購買是否完成的結果。

3. 廣告（Google AdMob）

免費版使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。AdMob 可能會收集並使用下列資料來提供、衡量及個人化廣告：

  • 裝置識別碼（例如廣告識別碼 IDFA，僅在您於「App 追蹤透明度」視窗允許後才會取得）
  • IP 位址與由此推估的大略位置
  • 裝置與系統資訊、廣告互動資料、當機與效能資料

歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者，會在第一次開啟時看到 Google 的同意聲明畫面。您也可以在 iPhone 的「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」關閉追蹤。拒絕追蹤不影響 App 的任何功能，只會看到非個人化廣告。購買完整版後不再顯示廣告。

Google 如何使用資料，請參考：Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料、Google 隱私權政策。

4. 資料刪除

刪除 App 即會移除所有儲存在裝置上的資料。

5. 兒童

本 App 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。

6. 政策變更與聯絡方式

本政策如有變更，會更新於本頁並修改上方日期。有任何問題請來信：lsyjim@hotmail.com

It's You Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

It's You ("the App") is developed by Lai Jiun Yung. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. Content you enter

The lists, options and settings you create are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer. The App has no user accounts and no developer-operated servers.

2. In-app purchases

The full version is sold through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Payment and billing are handled by Apple; the developer never receives your payment details, only whether a purchase succeeded.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect and use the following to serve, measure and personalize ads:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier IDFA, only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and system information, ad interaction data, crash and performance data

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland will see Google's consent message on first launch. You can turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining does not limit any feature; you will see non-personalized ads. Ads are removed after purchasing the full version.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Deletion

Deleting the App removes all data stored on your device.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes and contact

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Questions: lsyjim@hotmail.com
